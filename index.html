Botville

A personal assistant app that one person runs on their own computer.

Botville is a small pixel-art village where each robot does a job for its owner. One of them, Postmaster Pip, reads the owner's Gmail, sorts each new email by topic and importance, and writes a short digest twice a day.

It isn't a public service. There are no sign-ups and no other users. Botville runs on the owner's own desktop and is only reachable from the owner's own devices.

What it does with Gmail

  • Reads new inbox mail with read-only access.
  • Sorts each email (school, work, money, receipts, newsletters and so on) and flags anything urgent.
  • Writes a morning and an evening digest for the owner.
  • Only if the owner turns it on: saves suggested replies as drafts in Gmail after the owner approves each one. It never sends email.
